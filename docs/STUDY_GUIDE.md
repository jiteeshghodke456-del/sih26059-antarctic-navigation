# Study guide — where everything lives

Four documents, written to be fed to an AI assistant and studied in this
order. Every number in them cites the repo file that produces it.

1. **[study/EVOLUTION_AND_FINDINGS.md](study/EVOLUTION_AND_FINDINGS.md)** —
   how the project evolved stage by stage, every research finding with its
   evidence file, how the innovation is structural (honesty as architecture),
   and what is next.
2. **[study/ARCHITECTURE_AND_DATAFLOW.md](study/ARCHITECTURE_AND_DATAFLOW.md)** —
   the system end to end: file-by-file map, the five data pipelines with
   concrete paths, the grid story, and copy-pasteable commands for every data
   operation a reviewer might ask for.
3. **[study/PHYSICS_AND_PAPERS.md](study/PHYSICS_AND_PAPERS.md)** — every
   physical and statistical concept at interview depth: WDE17 drift, melt
   terms, passive-microwave retrieval and its QA traps, RMSE vs IIEE and the
   double-penalty effect, the residual U-Net, routing theory, and the full
   paper list.
4. **[study/OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md](study/OPERATIONS_RUNBOOK.md)** — every
   runnable operation (command → output → failure modes), the data
   inventory with what is irreplaceable, and the glossary.

Companions: [README.md](../README.md) (the front door),
[TRUE_CLAIMS.md](TRUE_CLAIMS.md) (every claim and its evidence, including the
withdrawn ones), [RESOLUTION_STUDY.md](RESOLUTION_STUDY.md) (F1/F3),
[backlog.md](backlog.md) (everything deferred, one line each).
